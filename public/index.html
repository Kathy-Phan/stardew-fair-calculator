Stardew Valley Grange Fair Calculator

This is a fan-made calculator, built to simplify calculating points in the Grange Display for the Stardew Valley Fair.

How to use:

  1. Search for your items
    • Use the search bar to type the name of items you plan to bring
    • Click or tap items to add them to your display list
    • Repeat until you’ve added all 9 items for your Grange Display

  2. Scroll down for your items
    • See below for all selected items
    • Look out for item quality . The higher the quality, the more points you get!
    • Remove/Replace items in the cart if needed

  3. Add your Professions
    • Click any relevant skills your character has.
    • Some items have more points depending on your profession. e.g.
    • If you're a Rancher, an iridium duck egg is worth 8pts instead of 7pts!

  4. Check your score!
    • Everything is automatically calculated for you!
    • If you don't trust it, just cross-reference it using the wiki.
    • Use this to optimise your score!
    • FYI: Mayor Lewis' shorts are NOT included. Use at your discretion!

Hope this is helpful!

This is a little passion project to help new stardew players not feel overwhelmed when calculating the grange fair display.
